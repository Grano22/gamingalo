# Phase 5: Ecosystem

**Goal:** grow the creator economy with a marketplace, a workshop, Forge and AI tools.

**Before starting:** get legal review of platform points, because they may be regulated as stored value. Reassess the order of these specs based on demand from existing game makers.

## Exit criteria

* [ ] A creator can list a free and a paid asset, and a game can load it after an entitlement check.
* [ ] Every marketplace item passes moderation and scanning, and takedowns work end to end.
* [ ] MCP lists available 3D models and their features.
* [ ] Points can't be purchased, spent or redeemed in any way the legal review ruled out.
* [ ] Games built by AI tools pass the same publishing checks as any other game.

## Risks

* Marketplaces bring copyright disputes, fraud and tax reporting duties.
* AI tools need licensing checks on generated content, and a clear cost per user.
